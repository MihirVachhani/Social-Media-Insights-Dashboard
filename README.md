# 📊 Social Media Insights Dashboard  

A dynamic, interactive analytics tool built in Excel to consolidate and visualize social media performance across platforms — focusing on post activity, audience growth, engagement, and optimal posting times.  

---

## 🌟 Short Description / Purpose  
The **Social Media Insights Dashboard** brings together data from **Instagram, X (Twitter), YouTube, Facebook, and LinkedIn** into one clear view. Based on **550 posts published between January 2024 and October 2024**, it helps recruiters, clients, and marketing teams quickly understand how their content is performing, where their audience is growing, and when to post for maximum impact.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
This dashboard was built using:  
- 📊 **Excel** – Main platform for dashboard creation and visualization.  
- 📈 **Pivot Tables** – For flexible analysis and dynamic aggregation.  
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Used for cleaning and reshaping data.  
- 🎨 **Conditional Formatting** – To highlight trends and performance thresholds.  
- 🧩 **KPI Cards, Slicers, Heatmaps, and Charts** – For interactive insights across platforms.  

---

## 📂 Data Source  
The dataset was **custom-generated and structured by the author**.  
- **Scope:** 550 posts across 5 platforms (Instagram, X, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn).  
- **Period:** January 2024 – October 2024.  
- **Structure:** 4 sheets with defined columns covering post type, impressions, engagement, follower growth, and posting time.  
- **Preparation:** Data refined using AI to ensure consistency and completeness.  

---

## 🔑 Features / Highlights  

**Business Problem**  
Companies, clients, and marketing teams often struggle to make sense of scattered social media data. Metrics like impressions, engagement, and follower growth vary across platforms, making it difficult to compare performance, identify trends, or decide when and what to post.  

**Goal of the Dashboard**  
The dashboard combines data from multiple sources into one interactive view. It delivers actionable insights by:  
- Tracking overall growth and engagement.  
- Comparing performance across platforms and content categories.  
- Highlighting trends in reach, impressions, and follower growth.  
- Revealing the best posting times to maximize engagement.  

**Key Visuals**  
- **KPI Cards:** Total Posts, Total Followers, Total Engagement, New Followers (Last 30 Days), Engagement (Last 30 Days).  
- **Posts per Platform (Column Chart):** Distribution of activity.  
- **Posts per Content Category (Pie Chart):** Breakdown by content type.  
- **Post Type vs Platform by Avg. Impressions (Clustered Column):** Content reach analysis.  
- **Content Category vs Platform by Avg. Engagement (Clustered Column):** Engagement quality comparison.  
- **Reach & Engagement Trend (Line Chart Combination):** Growth momentum over time.  
- **Net Growth of Followers by Month (Clustered Column):** Follower acquisition vs churn.  
- **Heatmap (Pivot Table):** Best posting times for engagement.  

**Business Impact**  
- **Recruitment Branding:** Showcase social media strength to recruiters and clients.  
- **Campaign Optimization:** Identify which platforms and content types deliver the highest ROI.  
- **Audience Growth Strategy:** Track follower trends and adjust posting frequency.  
- **Content Scheduling:** Use the heatmap to post at times proven to drive engagement.  
- **Video Insights:** For Reels, Shorts, and Videos, measure retention and replays to refine creative strategy.
